# P18 — London (Hackney Wick / Stratford, UK)
**Depends on:** P16 · **Refs:** D-22

## Goal
A data-rich foreign pilot that upgrades simulated layers with real open data where it exists.

## Steps
1. Register first: OSM (ODbL); **EA LIDAR Composite DTM 2022 1 m** (OGL v3) replaces Copernicus DEM; **EA Risk of Flooding from Surface Water** (OGL v3, *modelled by EA*) as a validation overlay — compare our extreme preset vs RoFSW extents (hit rate, labelled modelled-vs-modelled); **UKPN secondary sites** (coords + ONAN kVA rating; licence verified on the dataset page before use, else stay simulated) → transformers `data_status: observed` (locations/ratings) with simulated loads.
2. UK specifics: AQ index = **Defra DAQI** (1–10 bands) from CAMS Europe via Open-Meteo; design storm for simulated sewers = assumed UK practice — documented; transformer list 500/800/1000 kVA; timezone Europe/London; UTM 30N.
3. Full stack as P17 step 2; snapshots; audit.

## Acceptance
London region works end to end; RoFSW comparison shown with its own label; real UKPN transformers flagged observed; audit PASS.
