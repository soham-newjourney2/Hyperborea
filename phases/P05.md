# P05 — Weather + climate
**Day:** Sat 7 Nov · **Depends on:** P03 · **Refs:** DATA_SOURCES `om_forecast`, `om_archive`, `imd_thresholds`

## Goal
Live (cached) weather for the pilot, long-term rainfall climate context, and the rainfall scenario presets the disaster engine will use.

## Steps
1. `services/weather.py`: Open-Meteo forecast for the pilot centroid — current + hourly 72 h (precipitation, rain, temperature_2m, relative_humidity_2m, wind_speed_10m, precipitation_probability), `timezone=Asia/Kolkata`. httpx with 5 s timeout; cache 30 min in `local/cache/weather.json`; on failure serve last snapshot `data/snapshots/weather.json` with `stale=true` + `fetched_at`.
2. `pipelines/climate.py`: Open-Meteo archive (ERA5) daily precipitation_sum + temperature max/min, 1981 → last full month → `data/processed/climate_daily.csv`. Derive: annual total, monsoon (JJAS) total, annual max 1-day rain, days ≥ 64.5 mm (IMD heavy), ≥ 115.6 mm (very heavy), ≥ 204.5 mm (extremely heavy), simple linear trend (slope/decade + p-value) → `data/processed/climate_summary.json`. Label: *modelled reanalysis, not station observations*.
3. `config/scenarios.yaml` rainfall presets (simulated): `moderate` 15 mm/h × 3 h · `heavy` 40 mm/h × 2 h · `extreme` 80 mm/h × 2 h · `record_day` = ERA5 max 1-day total spread over 6 h. Each with description + source note.
4. Endpoints: `/weather/current`, `/weather/forecast`, `/climate/summary`, `/scenarios/presets`.
5. Frontend **Weather & Climate** panel: current conditions card (with source + fetched time + stale flag), 72 h rain bar chart (Recharts), climate charts (annual max 1-day rain bars with IMD lines; heavy-rain-days per year), presets list. Status-bar weather chip.
6. DATA_SOURCES entries; attribution "Weather data by Open-Meteo.com".

## Out of scope
Station data, downscaling, forecasting models.

## Acceptance (showcase)
Panel shows current weather + 72 h rain + 40-year rainfall context with source and time range; presets visible; works with network off (stale snapshot badge).

## Tests
Parse fixture JSON; fallback path when httpx raises; climate summary values non-negative and year range correct.
